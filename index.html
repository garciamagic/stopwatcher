Instructions

Part 1: The Four Ace Race

You race the clock to find the four aces. You don't beat your record, and you don't find the aces, but the four cards you pulled out match the stopwatch time exactly.

  1. Set up. Put a 2, 3, 4 and 5 in your pocket. Digits Mode always uses 2345.
  2. Open the app. It looks like a normal stopwatch at 00:00.00. Have them shuffle the deck, and ask them to time you finding the four aces.
  3. Race the clock. Put the deck in your pocket. They press Start. Pull out four cards face down, one at a time.
  4. Stop. They press Stop. It lands on a mix of your digits, like 00:34.52. You didn't beat your record.
  5. The reveal. Turn the cards over in the order the stopwatch shows (3, 4, 5, 2). They aren't the aces, but they match the time exactly. Finish by changing them into the four aces.

    It picks the mix closest to the real time, so nobody notices the change. Pulling out four cards takes about 20 to 30 seconds, which suits 2345 perfectly. Each time, it uses a different mix.

Part 2: Time Mode

Predict one exact time instead of digits.

  1. Turn on Time Mode. Digits Mode turns off by itself. Type a time, like 00:05.35. Tap + Add to save more times, and drag ≡ to put them in order. Each Stop uses the next time in the list.
  2. Let them time it. They press Start, then Stop after about 5 seconds. It stops on your exact time.

Part 3: Milliseconds Mode

The last two digits land on numbers you choose, one after another.

  1. Turn on Milliseconds Mode. Add your two-digit numbers, like 07, 21 and 52. Drag ≡ to change the order.
  2. Let them time it. They press Start, then Lap or Stop whenever they like.
  3. The reveal. Each Lap or Stop ends in your next number: .07, then .21, then .52.

    After the last number, it starts over from the first. Restart routine also starts it from the first number. Face-Down Mode doesn't work in this mode.

Part 4: Face-Down Mode

Optional. Works with Digits Mode or Time Mode.

  1. Turn on Face-Down Mode. Tap Allow if your phone asks about motion.
  2. Turn it over. After they press Start, have the phone placed face down: "Don't watch the clock, it makes me nervous."

    In Digits Mode, wait until at least 15 seconds have passed.

  3. Pick it up. Once your fourth card is out, turn the phone over. It has secretly jumped close to your force. They press Stop right away, and it lands on your force.

    In Time Mode, wait until you believe you're close to your set time before turning the phone over. Times after 20 seconds work well with this.

Part 5: Add it to your iPhone Home Screen

Do this once. The app then opens full screen, with no Safari bars, like a real app. Use Safari, not another browser.

  1. Open the app's web address in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button. It's a square with an arrow pointing up, in Safari's bottom bar. If you don't see it, tap the three dots first.
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen". It has a + in a square next to it. If you don't see it, tap View More and scroll down.
  4. Tap Add. Check that the name says Stopwatcher. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.
  5. Open it from the new icon on your Home Screen from now on.

Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen and choose Install (or tap Install app if you see it). It then opens full screen with no address bar.
